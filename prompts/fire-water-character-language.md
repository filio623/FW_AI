# Fire / Water Character Language

Use this file to converge on stable character descriptions.

## Jerry

Draft:

Small sentient fire character named Jerry, expressive flame body with no facial features, playful and brave through silhouette and motion, physically made of flame, bright core, orange/yellow edges, ember detail, casts warm light, clean silhouette.

Avoid:

- Full humanoid creature unless requested.
- Random monster features.
- Excessive smoke hiding the silhouette.
- Baked background when an element is needed.

## Sally

Draft:

Small sentient water droplet character named Sally, transparent blue body with no facial features, gentle personality through shape and motion, soft internal caustics, glossy surface, clean silhouette, lively squash-and-stretch motion.

Avoid:

- Ice creature.
- Generic blob without expressive shape language.
- Opaque plastic look unless requested.
- Baked background when an element is needed.

## Tom

Draft:

Large sentient fire villain named Tom, bigger and more dangerous than Jerry, aggressive flame silhouette with no facial features, hot bright core, deeper red/orange edges, darker smoke accents, strong glow, intimidating through shape and motion.

Avoid:

- Looking like only a scaled-up Jerry.
- Horror-monster details that break the film's tone.
- Smoke or turbulence that makes the body silhouette unreadable.
- Baked background when an element is needed.

## Wind Character

Draft:

Small helpful gust of wind character, semi-transparent curling wisps, light playful motion, friendly personality through shape and motion, no facial features, distinct from smoke and steam, clean silhouette.

Avoid:

- Looking like smoke from fire.
- Looking like steam from water.
- Being too invisible to read as a character.
- Baked background when an element is needed.
