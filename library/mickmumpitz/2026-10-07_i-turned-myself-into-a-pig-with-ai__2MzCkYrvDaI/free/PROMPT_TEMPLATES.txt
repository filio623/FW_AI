PROMPT TEMPLATES - H3 PERFORMANCE CAPTURE 2.2
=============================================


HOW THE PROMPT BOX WORKS
------------------------

One text, three parts, in this order:

  1. THE SWAP: what gets replaced and what stays. Copy it as is.
  2. THE CREATURE: what it looks like, how it is lit, how it acts. Fill the placeholders.
  3. THE TIMELINE (optional): timed cues for eyes, expressions, actions and dialogue.

Rules:
  - Keep <Picture 1> where it stands, that is your character sheet. With NO CHARACTER SHEET on,
    the workflow removes the <Picture 1> references by itself, so every template below also
    works without a sheet.
  - The sentence about the acting reference is added automatically. Do not write it.
  - Your voice drives the mouth. Writing the dialogue is optional; it helps the timing of expressions.
  - Never name your helmet, phone or rig in the prompt. Naming it makes H3 draw it.
    It belongs in WHAT GETS REPLACED.
  - Write in natural language and be descriptive. "Small head" in the prompt and a smaller
    GROW MASK both shrink a bobblehead.


WHICH TEMPLATE
--------------

  a creature head on your body              1          head, helmet, phone, camera rig on the helmet                   pose off
  your whole body as a real animal          2          person, helmet, phone, camera rig on the helmet                 pose off
  your whole body as a humanoid creature    3          person, helmet, phone, camera rig on the helmet                 POSE ON
  head and legs, torso stays                4          head, legs, trousers, shoes, helmet, phone, camera rig on the helmet   pose off
  one person out of several                 5          man (or woman)                                                  PERSON = who acts, 0 = leftmost

Placeholders, in CAPITALS inside square brackets:

  [CREATURE]   the creature, short                     a real horse / a green lizard / a pink domestic pig
  [LOOKS]      4 to 6 visual features, comma-separated  a short glossy chestnut coat, upright pointed ears, a long muzzle, big dark eyes
  [LIGHT]      the light of your shot                  warm late-afternoon sunlight from the right, soft shadows under the chin
  [ACTING]     what the face does, in verbs            his lips move big and clearly with every word, his ears flick, his eyes widen and squint
  [ACTION]     what the body does (full body only)     he creeps up to the planter and eats the plants

CHARACTER SHEET PROMPTS (Krea 2 Turbo, 1792 x 1024, 8 steps, cfg 1)
=====================================================================

Same idea: a layout sentence that never changes, a creature description you fill, and four emotion
heads that are as overacted as possible. Keep your real clothes in the text.

HUMANOID CREATURE (your body, a creature head)

A photorealistic character expression sheet on a plain light grey studio background, soft even studio lighting, sharp focus, realistic [fur] detail. The character is a humanoid [CREATURE]: a man's body with the head of [LOOKS]. His [furry] [CREATURE] neck grows out of his shirt collar. He wears [YOUR CLOTHES, e.g. a charcoal grey button-up cardigan over a black t-shirt, blue jeans and brown shoes], and has human hands. Layout: on the left a full-body front view of him standing straight; top middle a large head-and-shoulders side profile facing left; top right a large head-and-shoulders three-quarter view. Bottom row: four large head-and-shoulders portraits of the same [CREATURE], each with an extreme, overacted emotion like on an animator's expression sheet, but photorealistic. First, [EMOTION 1: laughing hysterically: the head tilted back, the mouth open as wide as possible showing the teeth and the tongue, the eyes squeezed shut into happy crescents]. Second, [EMOTION 2: sobbing and heartbroken: big shiny tears, the wet eyes squinting, the mouth open in a loud wail]. Third, [EMOTION 3: utterly disgusted: the nose scrunched up hard, the upper lip curled up, one eye squinted shut, the tongue sticking out]. Fourth, [EMOTION 4: furious and screaming: the mouth wide open with the teeth bared, the brows pulled down hard, the eyes narrowed to angry slits]. No emotion in the bottom row is mild or neutral; each one is readable from across the room. The same character in every view.
