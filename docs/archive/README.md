# Document Archive

Immutable snapshots of documents taken at a major revision, named `<NAME>-v<OLD_VERSION>.md`.

A snapshot lands here only when a canonical document undergoes a major revision. It is copied before the canonical document is replaced, carries `[ARCHIVED]` in its title and an `**Archived:**` date in its metadata block, and is never edited afterward except to correct an archival mistake.

Two things that look similar do **not** belong here:

- **Superseded frozen editions** such as `docs/jerry-character-bible_v001.md` stay at their original paths, because approved shots reference those paths directly. They are marked with `**Superseded by:**` instead.
- **Retired documents** with no successor stay at their original paths, marked `[ARCHIVED]`.

See the `Archiving Strategy` section of `AGENTS.md` for the full rules.
