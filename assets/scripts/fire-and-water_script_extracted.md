EXT. DAWN, FOREST The first morning light is gray. Cold air starts to warm as the sun creeps to barely peak over the horizon. The tiniest water droplets start to form on a leaf high in a tree. Two tiny droplets and then a third join together. The droplet, SALLY, has a smile on her face. Looking through watery eyes of the droplet that slowly grows, the colors of the forest become less gray and slightly more vivid and we see the edge of the sun as it breaks the black horizon. 

NEARBY A dying campfire’s burning embers feel the first ray of sunlight and it cracks. A spark flies in slow motion in a large arch. The spark is alive, HE is so excited. He looks right at Sally, and he, Jerry, is very alive and energetic, trying to impress her. Sally sees him, the spark Jerry, and she is in awe. Jerry looks to where he is going, a dried reed, and determination sets in as he gathers speed and braces for impact. He hits, and then there is silence. Then a smolder and a wisp of smoke. Suddenly a small flame. Jerry is victorious! He looks up to see her reaction, is she impressed?

She grows into the small puddle and now bigger, she looks up at him. Ashamed but with forlorn hope. He looks down at her and is at a loss, he hesitates and then tries to move towards her.
