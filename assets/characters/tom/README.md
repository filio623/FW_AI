# Tom Assets

Tom is the large fire villain character.

## Status

Draft/source-review phase. Do not put anything in `approved/` or `character-bible-v001/` until Robbie has reviewed and approved specific candidates.

## Folders

- `source-cgi/`: existing CGI renders, extracted stills, and proxy references of Tom or large fire.
- `higgsfield-candidates/`: generated stills or videos under review.
- `look-references/`: curated candidate references that may define Tom's body look, silhouette, material, scale, and movement language.
- `approval-review/`: curated review packs for Robbie's approval before promoting images into the bible.
- `character-bible-v001/`: assembled first-pass bible reference set after Robbie review.
- `approved/`: selected Tom visual anchors only after explicit approval.
- `expressions/`: emotion and face-layer references.
- `actions/`: pose and motion references.
- `training-candidates/`: reviewed images that may later be useful for LoRA, Soul, or other model setup.

Mirror durable identity decisions in `docs/character-bible.md` after review.
